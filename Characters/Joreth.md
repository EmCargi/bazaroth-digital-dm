# Joreth

**Race:** Hybrid — a breeding-program cross (insect/vampire/dragon leanings)
**Faction:** Maggot-Brood & Wraith-Speakers — free rider
**Role:** Infiltrator/wildcard — hell-drake rider
**Age:** 33

## Appearance

Half-insectoid, half-demon, with drake-scale plating along the spine. Rides a hell drake like a warhorse — the one creature the maggot-brood bred, not feared. Talks to his mount in a chittering hybrid tongue.

## Personality

Reckless, free, crew-loyal. A breeding-program hybrid with nothing to prove to the Infernal King — and everything to prove to the drakes. The wildcard who can pass anywhere the legions won't look.

## Background

Born in a demon lord's breeding vats, hatched with the drakes instead of ranked with the legion. Befriended a hell drake hatchling before anyone else dared. Now rides the Cauldron's skies as a free rider — the maggot-brood's envoy, the drakes' friend, and the Pilgrimage's most dangerous ally.

## Abilities

Hybrid resilience (radiation, cold), Hell-drake bond (ride the drakes), Insect-sense. Techniques: Two Weapons, Evasion, Lightning Reflexes. Skills: Military 2, Survival 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Vat-Born's Freedom — nothing binds him, so nothing anchors him.
- **Sixth Guard:** Drake-Severance — his mount is slain → the rider's whole world dies, and he falls into a suicidal dive.
- **Levers:** Drake-Dive / Chitin-Flurry / Vat-Burn
