# Morrow the Wraith-Speaker

**Race:** Wraith (bound spirit) — once a hellspawn, now a Shadowlands speaker
**Faction:** Maggot-Brood & Wraith-Speakers — bound to a demon lord
**Role:** Gatekeeper/lore — reads the waylines
**Age:** unknown (centuries)

## Appearance

Translucent, gaunt, eyes like banked coals. Bound by a demon lord's ritual, he drifts through the Shadowlands' gloom. Speaks in the whisper of a dozen ghosts at once.

## Personality

Cold, precise, patient. A wraith-speaker's gift is reading the energies between worlds — the waylines, the gates, the Cauldron's resonance. Bound to a demon lord's service, he has no free will but his knowledge is priceless.

## Background

A hellspawn who learned the Shadowlands' spirits too well and became one. Bound by the maggot-brood's strange magics to serve a demon lord. He can read the wayline to Earth through the Cauldron's blood — and he knows the Blight was bred here.

## Abilities

Wraith-bind (control spirits), Ethereal form, Sixth Sense (Waylines/Gates). Powers: Drain Energy, Supersense (Web). Skills: Occult 3, Academic 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Bound Will — a servant who cannot refuse.
- **Sixth Guard:** Unbinding — his demon lord is destroyed → he dissolves into the Shadowlands' spirits, the knowledge lost.
- **Levers:** Spirit-Whisper / Wayline-Read / Wraith-Veil
