# Korrath

**Race:** Hellspawn (psychic vampire)
**Faction:** Infernal King's Court — legion warrior
**Role:** Prime-faithful — the Pilgrimage of Bloods initiate
**Age:** 47

## Appearance

Horned, blood-red-eyed, a King's Legion Blade forged from the Cauldron's blood-metal slung across his back. The King's brand glows at his collarbone. Carries the hunger of a psychic vampire on a tight leash.

## Personality

Fierce, proud, sworn. The Pilgrimage of Bloods is his rite — retrieve a Fruit of the Trees of Death and earn a legion's rank. He believes the demons' destiny claim: they broke the Invaders, they hunt freely across the Web. Loyalty to the King is his spine; the brand is his soul's leash.

## Background

Born in a Dark Side citadel, raised in the legions, marked for the Pilgrimage by a demon lord who saw his hunger. The Cauldron's wayline hums when he's near it — and that hum frightens him more than the drakes.

## Abilities

Psychic Vampire (drain life force), Shape-shift (minor), Hell-drake sense. Techniques: Brutal, Lethal Blow. Skills: Military 3, Survival 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Hunger — feeding is a compulsion he barely masters.
- **Sixth Guard:** Brand-Severance — the King's brand stripped/defied → his oath collapses, the legion's soul is his no longer.
- **Levers:** Blood-Cleave / Hunger-Swarm / Legion Call
