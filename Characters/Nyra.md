# Nyra

**Race:** Asrai (winged — a lost angel from Aradia)
**Faction:** Captive — impaled-bound on a Tree of Death
**Role:** Moral thrall — free-or-use (cross-disc tie to Disc 8)
**Age:** 60

## Appearance

Winged but chained, an Asrai captive taken in a demon lord's raid. One wing clipped, the other bound. The Tree of Death's thorn waits for the verdict. Beautiful, defiant, afraid.

## Personality

Defiant, principled, fading. She's been a captive of Bazaroth for a year — the same world that bred the Bane her kin fight on Aradia. The party can free her (lose the leverage over a demon lord) or use her (keep the bargaining chip, carry the guilt).

## Background

A lost angel from Aradia — an Emissary whose Whirl-crossing landed her on Bazaroth. Taken by a demon lord who recognized her worth, bound at the Tree of Death where the worst enemies of the demon lords are impaled. Her wings are slow to re-form away from Aradia — she cannot flee on her own.

## Abilities

Flight (clipped — largely unusable), Healing (against her own wounds), Sixth Sense (Aradia's Whirl). Defects: Captive (bound), Impaired Wings.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Lost Angel's Longing — home is a world away and her wings won't carry her.
- **Sixth Guard:** Wing-Clip — her remaining wing is broken → she cannot flee, and her last hope dies.
- **Levers:** Aradia-Prayer / Thorn-Struggle / Whirl-Call
