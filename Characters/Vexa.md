# Vexa

**Race:** Hellspawn — snow-demon of the Faceless Queen (Moloch)
**Faction:** The 12 Demon Princes — Faceless Queen's enforcer
**Role:** Contrarian enforcer — the King's law in the Shadowlands
**Age:** 210

## Appearance

Pale as the Palace of Frozen Screams, medusa-tendrils framing a face that never blinks. A snow-demon's stillness; a law that doesn't flinch. The brand of the Faceless Queen is etched into her brow.

## Personality

Unbending, precise, loyal to the 12 princes' order. The King's law is her whole art — promotions by blood, fealty by brand. She's here to make sure the Pilgrimage stays lawful, even when the law is monstrous.

## Background

One of the Faceless Queen's enforcers, sent to the Shadowlands to guard the Pilgrimage's edge. She's watched a thousand initiates cross into the Bloom. She's never once considered not watching.

## Abilities

Psychic Vampire (negative-emotion feed), Snow-command (Control Environment: Cold), Medusa gaze. Techniques: Judge Opponent, Precise Aim. Skills: Military 3, Occult 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Law's Rigidity — the code is the only way to see the world.
- **Sixth Guard:** Fealty Crack — a prince she serves is executed → her obedience shatters, and the law dies with it.
- **Levers:** Snow-Interdict / Gaze-Censure / Legion Writ
