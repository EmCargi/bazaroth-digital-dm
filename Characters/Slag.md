# Slag

**Race:** Hellspawn — Bloom-changed (radiation-mutated)
**Faction:** Bloom Exiles — escaped slave
**Role:** Neutral fixer — knows the way across Dayside
**Age:** 88

## Appearance

Cracked, radiation-scarred hide, one eye gone violet from the Bloom. Carries a jury-rigged blade and the dead certainty of someone who survived the Cauldron's edge. The King's brand is burned out on his shoulder — a brand-severed exile.

## Personality

Wry, greedy, loyal to the escape. The Bloom is his home now; its paths, its monsters, its short-cuts are his trade. Wants the Pilgrimage to succeed because a legion's rank would let him leave — or buy his way back under the King.

## Background

An escaped slave from the Dark Side's vats, fled into the Bloom and survived long enough to know it. He's crossed the Plain of Despair a dozen times, dodged the maggot-brood, and watched the drakes nest. The Cauldron's blood-metal calls to his stolen blade.

## Abilities

Psychic Vampire (diminished), Bloom-blessed (radiation tolerance), Survival sense. Techniques: Concealment, Portable Armoury. Skills: Street 3, Survival 2, Mechanics 2.

## Active Narrative Syntax

- **Structural Fault:** The Escapee's Debt — he owes the Bloom his life and can't stop owing.
- **Sixth Guard:** Brand-Fever — the burned brand throbs when he nears the Cauldron → he flees the hunt rather than relive the brand.
- **Levers:** Bloom-Route / Slag-Trick / Ember-Cache
