# Bazaroth — The Demon Sun

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 318–319 (Bazaroth).

## Overview

Bazaroth is a **dying world** in a close orbit around a dim red dwarf star — the **Demon Sun**. It does not rotate relative to the sun: one side is in perpetual daylight (the **bloodlight**), the other locked in eternal darkness and cold. The proximity causes tidal effects that shake the world: massive quakes, chasms opening with geysers of hot steam or molten lava, and chains of volcanoes streaming rivers of lava across the land.

On the Darkside, the fitful glow of volcanoes and lava pools is all that supports life.

## The Hellspawn

The denizens of this hellish land are the **hellspawn**, often called demons by those who encounter them. Many tribes, diverged greatly by the hellish environment. Most are humanoid, centauroid, or serpentine; many more powerful demons can change shape or size. All are predators, and most are **psychic vampires** — gaining nourishment not merely from eating, but from the extinguishing of life force itself.

The demons' **breeding programs** (deliberately sustained for the last thousand years) create hybrids and nurture "dark lords" / "harbingers of the apocalypse" on other worlds. **One such entity was the Bane** that destroyed the fairy world Arcadia — the same Bane Aradia fights (Disc 8).

## The Cosmology

The hellspawn claim they once defeated an army of **Invaders from Beyond** that terrified the Multiverse, and that the Weaver rewarded them with the right to hunt freely across the Cosmic Web. The Asrai of Aradia dispute this — they say the demons *opened* the Cosmic Web to the Invaders and only realized their folly too late. The current Infernal King does not openly challenge strong worlds; he probes weaknesses.

## Cast Hook

The demo's **Pilgrimage of Bloods** — a Darkside rite of passage into the Day Side to retrieve a Fruit of the Trees of Death.
