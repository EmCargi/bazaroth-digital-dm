# The Pilgrimage of Bloods & The Cosmic Web

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, p. 319 (Bazaroth).

## The Pilgrimage of Bloods

In some Darkside tribes, it is the custom to send young hellspawn into the Day Side to perform a quest — retrieving one of the **Fruits of the Trees of Death**, or the skulls of 13 other demons. This is the **Pilgrimage of Bloods**, a common rite of passage and required to enter one of the greater Legions.

## Bazaroth's Waylines

Bazaroth has **seven waylines**: one to Earth, two to Inner Worlds, and four to all the other Prime Worlds save Aradia. One of the gates on the wayline to Earth is in the **Citadel of Fire**. The Infernal King sits on the Council of Omphalos.

## The Contested History

The hellspawn claim destiny: they defeated the **Invaders from Beyond**, and the Weaver rewarded them with the right to hunt freely across the Cosmic Web. The Asrai of Aradia dispute this — they say the demons opened the Web to the Invaders first. Whatever the truth, the demons are perfectly aware of other dimensions, and individual demon lords have their own gates and schemes.

## Cast Hook

The demo's lore node is the **Cauldron** — where the wayline resonance hums beneath the blood, and where the party learns the Blight the Aradians fight was bred here (cross-disc tie).
