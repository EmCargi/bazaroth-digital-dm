# The Dayside & The Bloom

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, p. 319 (Bazaroth).

## The Shadowlands

The Shadowlands lie between the Day and Night Sides — a narrow (few hundred km) band of perpetual cool twilight, feared for its **wraiths**: energy-draining ghosts, banshees, and cannibal spirits. Some demon lords know rituals to bind these creatures as servants. The demo's journey starts here.

## Dayside & Antaris

Across the Shadowlands, the blood-red sun hangs overhead and the sky is purple and orange. Most of Dayside is ocean; in its centre is the continent of **Antaris**, directly facing the eye of the sun at a stifling 50°C. Here vegetation grows — black, crimson, or sickly-purple leaves.

## The Bloom

Antaris and the islands are dominated by the **Bloom** — endless jungles and swamp teeming with life and death. The unstable, flaring radiation of the Demon Sun causes rapid mutation: species constantly evolve, becoming more deadly. The Bloom is alive with monstrous hellbeasts — three-headed worms the size of subway trains, carnivorous trees, armoured and spiked behemoths, floating air-jellyfish, swarms of flesh-eating butterflies, clouds of vampire pixies, and the hell-bunny whose mouth opens to reveal tentacles. Even demons fear the Bloom.

## The Plain of Despair

Toward Antaris' centre lies a massive ring of volcanic mountains — the **Knives of the Sun** — beyond which looms the **Plain of Despair**: a red desert where the hellspawn defeated an army of Invaders from Beyond. Scattered about are the dismembered corpses of weirdly beautiful giants, some a hundred metres tall, decaying with preternatural slowness. The half-insectoid **maggot-brood** of Antaris dwell within the corpses, practicing strange magics feared even by darksiders.

## The Cauldron & Trees of Death

At the Plain's centre is the **Cauldron of Bazaroth** — the hottest place on the world, a sea of bubbling blood feeding rivers through the Pain Forest. Rising from the sea are the **Trees of Death**: 13 gigantic trees, hundreds of metres high, whose roots drink the blood and upon whose thorns are impaled the worst enemies of the demon lords (captured Asrai, for example). Death-Tree fruit has powerful necromantic and alchemical properties. Nesting in the branches are **hell drakes** — hybrids of insect, vampire, and dragon.

## Cast Hook

**Slag** (escaped Bloom slave) knows the way; **Joreth** (hybrid drake-rider) hunts the drakes.
