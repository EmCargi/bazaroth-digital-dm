# The Dark Side & The Citadel of Fire

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, p. 318 (Bazaroth).

## The Dark Side

The Dark Side of Bazaroth forever hides its face from the sun. In its centre is **Moloch** — a half-frozen continent thrusting out of the ice: a wasteland of fire and cold, endless plains of ice and rock, enormous volcanoes belching soot and lava. The continent is riddled with ancient lava tubes, great subterranean vaults, and tunnel complexes heated by lava pools and steam geysers. Strange fungi, slimes, and oozes grow there — some naturally, others cultivated by slaves in bubbling vats.

## The Sea of Tears

Much of the Dark Side is ocean — the **Sea of Tears**, largely frozen. Its depths conceal great leviathans (island-sized horrors), flesh-eating mermaids, and similar terrors. Volcanic islands thaw patches of ice; larger islands host demonic citadels of black obsidian or the shells and bones of deep-sea creatures.

## The Infernal King & The Citadel of Fire

The ruler of the Dark Side is the **Infernal King**, ruling from the **Citadel of Fire** — a black glass city carved from obsidian resembling a hellish Venice, with canals of molten lava and bridges of red-hot iron. The King sits on the **Council of Omphalos** (sometimes sending a trusted demon lord as ambassador). One of the gates on the wayline to Earth is in the Citadel of Fire.

## The 12 Demon Princes

In Moloch's chilly centre is the **Palace of Frozen Screams**, home of the medusa-like **Faceless Queen** of the snow-demons — one of the 12 demon princes/princesses who owe fealty to the Infernal King. Each rules their own slice of hell. Promotions are earned by blood — duels when a leader dies, is executed, or is assassinated.

## Cast Hook

**Korrath** (Infernal King's legion warrior) — the Pilgrimage initiate. **Vexa** (Faceless Queen's snow-demon) — the King's law.
