# The Bloom Exiles & Escaped Slaves

**Type:** Desperate exiles, escaped slaves, and rebels against the Infernal King
**Home:** The Bloom — Dayside jungles and swamp, 50°C, constant mutation
**Doctrine:** Survival over loyalty — the Bloom is the only place a slave can hide

Even demons fear the Bloom, but it is the only refuge for those who flee the King. Escaped slaves, exiles, and rebels dwell in the mutated jungle, knowing its paths and its monsters. Some have been there so long the radiation has changed them.

**Cast hook:** **Slag** (escaped Bloom slave, `[Brand: Exile]`) — the neutral fixer who knows the way across Dayside.
