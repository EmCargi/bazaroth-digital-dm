# The 12 Demon Princes & The Faceless Queen

**Ruler:** The Faceless Queen — medusa-like snow-demon of the Palace of Frozen Screams, Moloch
**Rank:** One of the 12 demon princes/princesses owing fealty to the Infernal King
**Rule:** Each rules their own slice of hell — an island or subterranean vault

The 12 princes are the infernal aristocracy. The Faceless Queen commands the snow-demons of Moloch. Beneath them are countless lesser demon lords, each with their own legion of aristocratic warriors. Hellspawn vie for positions in the most powerful legions — promotions earned by blood.

**Cast hook:** **Vexa** (snow-demon of the Faceless Queen, `[Brand: Enforcer]`) — the King's law in the Shadowlands.
