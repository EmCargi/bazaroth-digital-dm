# The Maggot-Brood & Wraith-Speakers

**Home:** The corpses of the Invaders on the Plain of Despair, Antaris
**Reputation:** Strange magics, feared even by darksiders
**Allies:** Some demon lords bind wraiths as servants; a few hellspawn learn the Shadowlands' spirits

The **maggot-brood** of Antaris are half-insectoid barbarians dwelling within the dismembered corpses of the Invaders, practicing strange magics. They are a law unto themselves on the Plain.

The **wraith-speakers** are hellspawn (or bound spirits) who understand the Shadowlands' wraiths, banshees, and cannibal spirits — the ones who can read the energies between worlds.

**Cast hook:** **Morrow the Wraith-Speaker** (`[Brand: Seer]`) — the gatekeeper/lore, bound to a demon lord but able to read the waylines.
