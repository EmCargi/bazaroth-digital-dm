# The Infernal King's Court

**Ruler:** The Infernal King — the Citadel of Fire (obsidian Venice, lava canals)
**Council:** Sits on the Council of Omphalos (sometimes via ambassador)
**Doctrine:** Probe weak worlds; don't openly challenge the strong Primes
**Law:** Promotions by blood — duels on any leader's death

The Infernal King rules the Dark Side's hellspawn, and claims the right to hunt across the Cosmic Web (contested by Aradia's Asrai). The King doesn't openly challenge strong worlds — he probes weaknesses, and individual demon lords scheme with their own gates.

**Cast hook:** **Korrath** (legion warrior, `[Brand: Initiate]`) — the Pilgrimage of Bloods under the King's law.
