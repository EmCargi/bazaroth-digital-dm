# Hellspawn Powers & The Blood-Steel Blade

> **Source:** BESM 4e Chapter 14, pp. 318–319 (Bazaroth).

## Hellspawn Racial Traits

- **Psychic Vampire** — feed on life force, not just food; some feed on negative emotions.
- **Shape-shift / interbreed** — many powerful demons change shape or size; breeding programs make hybrids.
- **Tribes** — humanoid, centauroid, serpentine; the maggot-brood half-insectoid.
- **Way-Bound** — powerful demons are Way-Bound, summonable via true-name ritual at a gate.

## Powers → card_json

Psychic Vampire, Shape-shift, Wraith-bind, Hell-drake ride, Necromantic fruit-sense all have **no DB column** — they live in `card_json.powers` + the `race` column as narrative, injected as prose (the established Enid/Cathedral/Ikaris/Aradia pattern). The loadout DB columns carry techniques/skills/defects + Narrative Syntax.
