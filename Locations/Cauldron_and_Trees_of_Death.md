# The Cauldron & Trees of Death — Bazaroth's Heart

**Type:** The hottest place on the world — a sea of bubbling blood
**Hook:** The 6-node starter's lore node (`node_04_cauldron`)

At the Plain of Despair's centre, the Cauldron of Bazaroth bubbles — a sea of blood feeding rivers through the Pain Forest. Rising from it are the **Trees of Death**: 13 gigantic trees whose roots drink the blood, upon whose thorns are impaled the worst enemies of the demon lords (captured Asrai). Death-Tree fruit has powerful necromantic and alchemical properties. Hell drakes nest in the branches.

**The Wayline Resonance:** Beneath the blood, the wayline to Earth (via the Citadel of Fire) hums — the party can feel the Cosmic Web. The lore reveal: the Blight the Aradians fight was bred here.

**Mechanics:** A `lore` node with a wraith-speaker's read — reach it, and the party learns Bazaroth's true place in the Multiverse.
